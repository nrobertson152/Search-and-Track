# Debrief, Career and Alert Duty

> How the shift is judged, and what comes after it

## The debrief

When the watch ends, the after-action report opens:

- **Replay.** The whole shift on the display, with the truth shown: every aircraft that was really there, where it really went, and when your radars first saw it. Play it, scrub through it, stop it.
- **Objectives** and their status, with points and penalties.
- **Detection and identification** for each aircraft: first plot, first track, identity and when it was given, and why it was missed if it was.
- **Expenditure and losses.**
- **The newspaper.** What the world reads about your shift the next morning.

## Grades

US: OUTSTANDING (90% and up), EXCELLENT (75%), SATISFACTORY (55%), MARGINAL (35%), UNSATISFACTORY.

PVO: 5 EXCELLENT (85%), 4 GOOD (70%), 3 SATISFACTORY (50%), 2 UNSATISFACTORY.

Penalties count against the score: a civil loss or a fratricide can sink an otherwise perfect shift.

## Career

The campaign is a service record. Watches unlock in historical order, starting with the three training lessons. Each result is stamped COMMENDATION or REPRIMAND; your best score for each watch counts toward your career points, and your rank follows them, from Second Lieutenant (Лейтенант) to Colonel (Полковник). The record is kept in your data folder and survives between sessions.

## Battles and the war

Battles end in VICTORY (70% of the objectives' points and up), DRAW (40%) or DEFEAT, and always DEFEAT if what you were there to protect is sunk. The debrief adds what each side lost, the anti-ship and cruise missiles fired and what became of them, and every decision with who made it. In a war, each battle is booked into the war diary and its losses carry into the next; the war as a whole is a VICTORY at 65% of all the points, a DEFEAT under 40% or if the force loses what it cannot fight without.

## Alert Duty

A procedurally generated shift on a real order of battle: choose a sector (each with the forces and era of a historical watch), choose QUIET, ROUTINE or BUSY, and take the watch. Peacetime rules. Expect civil traffic on and off its flight plans, stragglers, lost light aircraft, probes along the border, low intruders, and the equipment and line failures of an ordinary night. NEW SEED rolls a different shift. Every Alert Duty watch can be saved and resumed like any other.

## Saving

`F5` saves the shift in progress, `F9` loads it. Saves, settings and the career record live in your data folder: `~/.local/share/searchtrack` on Linux (or `$XDG_DATA_HOME/searchtrack`), `%APPDATA%\searchtrack` on Windows. Nothing is sent anywhere: the game is entirely offline.
